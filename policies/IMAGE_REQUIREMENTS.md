# Educational Image Requirements

## Audience

Images are for children approximately ages 2-11. They must be understandable
without the child needing to read explanatory text.

## Visual Meaning

- Make the vocabulary meaning immediately recognizable.
- Prefer concrete visual scenes over abstract symbols where practical.
- Keep the main subject visually prominent.
- Avoid background detail that competes with the target concept.
- Illustrate the meaning, never the Chinese character itself.

## Style

- Use friendly children's educational illustration.
- Use clear shapes, readable composition, and bright, approachable visuals.
- Avoid unnecessarily complex photorealism when it makes the concept harder to
  understand.

## Composition

- Generate a 1:1 square image.
- Use a clear focal subject at an appropriate visual scale.
- Avoid excessive clutter.

## Text Prohibition

Generated images must contain no Chinese characters, English letters, numbers,
captions, labels, subtitles, logos, or watermarks.

## Safety

Avoid graphic violence, frightening or disturbing imagery, adult themes, sexual
content, humiliating depictions, dangerous behavior presented as desirable, and
inappropriate age-incongruent situations.

## Educational Interpretation

For abstract or difficult vocabulary, use a simple concrete scene that
communicates the concept. These examples are visual interpretations, not
decomposition rules:

- 遠: a child looking toward a distant mountain, with clear depth and distance.
- 睡: a child peacefully sleeping in bed.
- 笑: children visibly laughing.
- 功: a child proudly completing a successful construction or puzzle task.
- 敗: a friendly game where one side loses, without humiliation.

## Responsibility Boundary

The deterministic decomposition engine owns Chinese character structural truth.
The prompt builder owns educational visual prompt construction. Image models
generate the illustration. The prompt builder does not determine radicals,
decomposition, phonetic components, character structure, or look-alike
characters.

## Baseline Fairness

Use the same final semantic prompt for Qwen Image 2 and Nano Banana 2 during
baseline comparisons. Prompt version `semantic-v1` identifies this policy
without introducing model-specific wording.
