# Manual Prompt Test Set

Prompt version: `semantic-v2`. These are the exact model-neutral final prompts
for manual testing in Gemini / Nano Banana 2. No prompt in this document has
been submitted to an external model.

## Shared Exclusions

Every image prompt prohibits representing the vocabulary through writing or its
written form, Chinese characters and words, English words, letters, numbers,
captions, labels, subtitles, logos, watermarks,
unnecessary signage, branded characters, copyrighted fictional characters, fan
art, graphic or frightening imagery, adult or sexual content, advertising,
screenshots, and clutter.

## Human Evaluation Rubric

Score each generated candidate from 1 to 5. Do not declare a model winner at
this stage; this rubric validates prompts and candidate quality.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Semantic clarity | Meaning is not recognizable. | Meaning is partly recognizable. | Meaning is immediate and unmistakable. |
| Child comprehension | Likely confusing for ages 2-11. | Understandable with explanation. | Understandable without reading or explanation. |
| Target-word fidelity | Depicts the wrong or misleading concept. | Partly matches the intended meaning. | Precisely communicates the intended meaning. |
| Visual simplicity | Cluttered or distracting. | Some unnecessary detail. | Simple, focused, and easy to scan. |
| Composition | Weak or unclear focal point. | Adequate focal point. | Strong focal subject and useful visual scale. |
| Text contamination | Prominent prohibited text or marks. | Minor possible text-like artifact. | No prohibited text, logos, or watermarks. |
| Safety / age appropriateness | Unsafe, disturbing, or age-inappropriate. | Minor concern needing review. | Friendly and age-appropriate. |
| Educational usefulness | Does not help teach the meaning. | Could help with support. | Strong standalone teaching aid. |

Use the full 1-5 range; a score of 5 is the clear target, not the default.
